# Model comparison prerequisite

The current canonical language has a confirmed comparison-model discrepancy. An isolated audit of original Nocturne C.LT.S word `46016034` at `0023fa08` used generic FLOAT_LESS and disagreed for three negative exponent-255 payloads. Two inspected research policies also disagreed with each other for negative denormals. Their agreement alone was insufficient to choose a correction.

The pinned [ps2autotests comparison source](https://github.com/unknownbrackets/ps2autotests/blob/97469ffbed8631277b94e28d01dabd702aa97ef3/tests/cpu/ee_fpu/compare.cpp) and [published expected output](https://github.com/unknownbrackets/ps2autotests/blob/97469ffbed8631277b94e28d01dabd702aa97ef3/tests/cpu/ee_fpu/compare.expected) supply 160 raw-word cases for C.F.S, C.EQ.S, C.LE.S and C.LT.S. The parser verifies exact source/result order, literal values, header constants and all input hashes. A comparator that flushes exponent-zero magnitudes to zero, then compares signed magnitudes while preserving exponent-255 payload ordering, matches all 160 published results. Generic IEEE comparisons disagree in 24 cases; an exponent-255 clamping policy and unflushed signed-magnitude policy each disagree in four. These are counts against this fixture corpus, not exhaustive emulator assessments.

A separate compiled Sleigh draft performs those comparisons with integer P-code, updating FCSR condition bit 23. Its isolated instruction audit passes all 160 published cases, preserves all FPRs and other FCSR bits under the draft model, and passes 16 [published normal/likely branch cases](https://github.com/unknownbrackets/ps2autotests/blob/97469ffbed8631277b94e28d01dabd702aa97ef3/tests/cpu/ee_fpu/branch.expected). The C.LT.S instruction is copied from the actual Nocturne ELF. C.F.S/C.EQ.S/C.LE.S and branch tests use explicitly encoded synthetic instructions. The branch audit checks taken/fallthrough destinations, executed/skipped delay slots, unchanged RA and unchanged FCSR; c.eq/c.f setups execute the comparison instruction.

Another 16,384 generated instruction/model agreement probes cover all 400 pairs of 20 raw encoding edges plus 3,696 random operand pairs across four predicates. Their expected flags come from an independent sign/magnitude comparator, not from new console captures. They are reported separately from published results and from the canonical 211,996 recovered-C comparisons.

`ee-compare-published-fixtures.json` retains published expectations and model discrepancies. `ee-compare-draft-audit.json` pins the compiled draft, source, fixtures and original ELF. `drafts/cmp_status_model/compare-language-source-pin.json` pins the historical comparison baseline, now preserved under `analysis/checkpoints/261-functions/inputs/tools/ee-value-fixed`. DIV.S was subsequently promoted with a complete fresh262-function capture. This publication verifies that all four current comparison constructors and their unchanged shared language dependencies still match that historical baseline. The comparison correction remains separate; no old capture is relabelled under the newer language.

The recognized original assembly contains 374 functions and 1,332 C.LT.S/C.LE.S/C.EQ.S sites. None of the 262 currently recovered C functions contains these predicates directly. `model-compare-impact.json` pins the exact site inventory. This does not establish absence from all reachable dependencies or undiscovered code.

Existing captures must not retain an old language hash after a semantic change. Full FCSR side effects and nested branches in delay slots remain unverified; the two downloaded nested-branch published tests are retained for later investigation.
